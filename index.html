About TGMS

TGMS is an independent automotive strategy advisory led by Naoki Tomura, with 35 years of experience across US, Japanese and Chinese OEMs.

We focus on high-granularity, data-driven insights to support global OEMs and suppliers in:

  • Market Entry & Localization Strategy
  • ASEAN & Japan Regulatory / Certification Navigation
  • Volume, Pricing, and Segmentation Analytics
  • Competitor & Portfolio Tracking
  • Executive-level strategic briefing decks

Based in Japan, operating globally.
